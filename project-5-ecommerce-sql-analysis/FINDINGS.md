# E-commerce Sales — Findings & Recommendations

**To:** Head of Sales
**From:** ZEN, Data Analyst
**Re:** Revenue performance, top categories and customers

---

## Bottom line

The business generated **$1,803,688** across **3,000 orders**, but it's **highly concentrated**: Electronics alone accounts for **~half of all revenue ($904,431)**. That's a strength to leverage and a concentration risk to manage.

## What I found

- **Total revenue:** $1,803,688 from 3,000 orders.
- **Revenue by category:** Electronics $904,431 (≈50%), Sports $333,511, Home $313,849, Apparel $187,589, Books $64,309.
- **Top customer** spent $10,893 — a small number of high-value customers are worth protecting.

## Interpretation

Electronics is the engine of the business. Doubling down (range, promotion, stock) could grow revenue fastest — but the heavy reliance means a dip in Electronics would hit the whole business. Books contribute little and may not be worth the same shelf/marketing investment.

## Recommendations

1. **Invest in Electronics** (the proven driver) while **diversifying** the next-tier categories (Sports, Home) to reduce concentration risk.
2. **Build a VIP programme** for top customers like the one spending $10,893.
3. **Review low-revenue categories** (Books) — reallocate marketing toward higher-return categories.
