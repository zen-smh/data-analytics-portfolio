# Customer Segmentation — Findings & Recommendations

**To:** Marketing team
**From:** ZEN, Data Analyst
**Re:** RFM customer segments and how to target each

---

## Bottom line

Of 600 customers, **216 (36%) are "at risk"** — they used to buy but have gone quiet — making re-engagement the single biggest opportunity. At the other end, **132 Champions (22%) drive the most value (avg $1,594 each)** and must be protected.

## What I found

| Segment | Customers | Avg spend | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| At risk | 216 | $322 | Lapsing — win them back |
| Loyal | 135 | $726 | Steady — grow them |
| Champions | 132 | $1,594 | Best — retain & reward |
| Needs attention | 66 | $336 | Middling |
| New / Promising | 51 | $227 | Recent — convert to repeat |

## Interpretation

The "at risk" group is both the largest and the most actionable: these are people who already liked us enough to buy, so winning them back is cheaper than acquiring new customers. Champions are few but disproportionately valuable — losing even a handful hurts.

## Recommendations

1. **Launch a win-back campaign** for the 216 at-risk customers (a personalised offer or "we miss you" message). This is the top priority.
2. **Create a VIP/loyalty tier** for the 132 Champions to lock in retention.
3. **Onboard New/Promising customers** with a second-purchase nudge.
4. Stop one-size-fits-all email — tailor by segment.
