## Bottom line

The chain generated **$6,067,370** across **39,060 units**, and performance is **remarkably even** across both stores and categories — no single store or product line dominates or lags badly. The new dashboard lets you explore all of it yourself, filtered by region, store and category.

## What I found

- **Total revenue:** $6,067,370 from 39,060 units.
- **By category:** Electronics leads modestly ($1.25M), then Grocery, Home, Beauty, Apparel — all within ~$1.17–1.22M. A balanced mix.
- **By store:** George St top ($1.27M); all five stores fall within a narrow band (~$1.17–1.27M).

## Interpretation

A balanced spread is healthy — the business isn't over-reliant on one store or category, which lowers risk. But "even" can also mean no standout strength, so growth would come from lifting the whole network rather than fixing a single weak point.

## Recommendations

1. Use the dashboard as the standing weekly view — it removes ad-hoc number requests.
2. Since no store is failing, focus growth on **chain-wide levers** (range, pricing, promotion) rather than rescuing one site.
3. Watch the monthly trend in the dashboard for emerging seasonal patterns and act on stock/staffing ahead of them.
